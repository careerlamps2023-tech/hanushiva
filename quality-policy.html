Quality Policy

What we check, when we check it, and what happens when something is wrong.

Our Commitment

Consistent quality, honestly described

Hanushiva Impex is committed to supplying goods that consistently meet the agreed specification — and to describing every consignment exactly as it is.

Consistency

Every repeat order is matched against the approved reference sample, so species, grade and finish do not drift between shipments.

Honest Grading

Timber is graded to declared standards and moisture content is measured, not estimated. We do not upgrade a species or grade in order to win an order.

Verified Vendors

Sawmills, panel plants and craft units are audited for capacity, drying facilities and compliance before they join our panel.

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Each consignment is measured, moisture-tested and photographed at the mill before it is sealed for dispatch.

Compliance

Phytosanitary, fumigation, ISPM-15 and all customs requirements are met and documented in full.

Continuous Improvement

Customer feedback and rejection data are reviewed periodically and fed back to the vendors concerned.

Control Points

Six checkpoints on every order

  • Vendor assessment and approval before the first order
  • Reference sample approved and retained by both parties
  • In-process check during production
  • Final random inspection and moisture check on the finished lot
  • Bundling, marking and tally verification at loading
  • Document check before submission to the bank
“Consistency is not an inspection result. It is the discipline of comparing every lot to the same approved sample.”

Accountability

If something goes wrong

No inspection regime is perfect. What matters is what happens next.

How quickly will you respond to a complaint?
Every quality complaint is acknowledged within one working day of receipt.
What do you need from us?
Photographs of the affected material, the bundle or carton markings, and the invoice reference. That is normally enough for us to take the matter up with the mill.
What resolutions are possible?
Depending on the case: replacement in the next shipment, a credit note, or a rate adjustment on the affected quantity. We tell you which one applies and why.
Does the same problem recur?
Complaints and their outcomes are recorded and reviewed with the vendor, so the same issue does not reappear in a later shipment.
