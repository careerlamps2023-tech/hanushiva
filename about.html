About Us

A New Delhi based timber and wood products trading house that treats your specification as a commitment, not a suggestion.

Who We Are

Hanushiva Impex

Hanushiva Impex LLP is a Limited Liability Partnership registered under the laws of India, with its principal place of business at First Floor, 822 Temple Road, Bhogal, Jangpura, New Delhi – 110014.

The firm is registered with the Directorate General of Foreign Trade as an importer and exporter, and is registered under GST in Delhi.

We deal in sawn timber, plywood, pinewood and wooden home decor items. Buyers come to us with a specification; we come back with vetted mills and craft units, verified samples, a landed cost and a delivery schedule we can stand behind.

The business runs in two directions. We import and export internationally, and we trade domestically on a Pan India basis — distributing to timber merchants, furniture and joinery units, panel distributors and project contractors in all Indian states.
