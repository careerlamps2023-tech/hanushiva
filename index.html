Who We Are

A timber trading house built on trust

Hanushiva Impex LLP is a Limited Liability Partnership based in New Delhi, registered with the Directorate General of Foreign Trade as an importer and exporter.

We deal in solid wood, panel products and finished wooden articles — supplying export buyers overseas and, through our local trade and distribution arm, merchants and manufacturers Pan India. Species, grade and moisture content are declared before dispatch, not discovered at the door.

  • Species selection matched to the end use
  • Grading and moisture content declared per bundle
  • Pre-shipment inspection with photographic reports
  • Complete statutory export documentation
  • Local trade and distribution, Pan India — all Indian states

What We Do

End-to-end trade services

Everything between your enquiry and your warehouse, handled by one team with one point of contact.

Export Services

Sourcing, grading and export of timber and wood products on FOB, CFR or CIF terms to buyers worldwide.

Import Services

Import of sawn timber, logs, plywood and pinewood with full customs and compliance handling.

Vendor Sourcing

Identifying, auditing and shortlisting sawmills and panel plants that match your specification.

Quality Inspection

Species, grade and moisture-content checks with photographic reports before goods leave the mill.

Custom Packing

Bundling and strapping, fumigation, private labelling and carton marking per destination norms.

Documentation

Invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, phytosanitary and ISPM-15 paperwork.

Freight & Logistics

FCL, LCL and air freight through established forwarders, with tracking until delivery.

Consolidation

Multiple vendors combined into one container to cut your landed cost per unit.
