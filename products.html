01 / Solid Wood

Sawn Timber

Tropical hardwood logs sawn to the sizes your production line actually uses — square-edged, end-sealed and bundled by thickness. We supply both air-dried and kiln-dried stock, and we state the moisture content on the packing list rather than leaving you to find out at the door.

Common applications include structural framing, door and window joinery, furniture components, decking and heavy-duty outdoor construction. Species selection depends on where the wood will sit: exposed or covered, load-bearing or decorative.

  • Square-edged sawn boards, scantlings and beams
  • Air-dried (18–20% MC) or kiln-dried (10–12% MC)
  • Bundled and strapped by thickness, ends sealed
  • Grading and moisture content declared per bundle
Thickness25 mm – 100 mm (1″ – 4″)
Width75 mm – 300 mm (3″ – 12″)
Length6 ft – 14 ft, or cut to buyer's list
Moisture ContentAir-dried 18–20% · Kiln-dried 10–12%
FinishRough sawn, or planed on request

Timber Species

Species we handle

Each species earns its place for a reason — hardness, movement, colour or resistance to weather and insects. Tell us the end use and we will recommend the one that suits it.

Nyatoh

A medium-density hardwood with a fine, even texture and a warm pinkish-brown tone. Works and finishes cleanly, which makes it a standing favourite for furniture, doors and cabinetry.

Meranti

Light to dark red, moderately hard and easy to machine. The general-purpose choice for interior joinery, mouldings, furniture frames and plywood cores.

Kapur

A dense, straight-grained hardwood with a rich reddish-brown colour and good natural durability. Holds up well in both covered and exposed positions.

Merbau

Very hard and heavy, with an interlocked grain and deep reddish-brown colour. Naturally resistant to termites and weather — the usual pick for decking and flooring.

Chengal

One of the most durable tropical hardwoods available — high strength, excellent weather resistance and a long service life outdoors. Used for heavy structures, bridges and exposed decking.

Other Species on Request

Kasah, Balau, Keruing and other tropical hardwoods can be sourced against a firm enquiry. Tell us the specification, quantity and end use, and we will confirm availability and price.

02 / Panel Products

Plywood

Cross-laminated panels in commercial, water-resistant and marine grades. We supply full sheets and cut sizes, in calibrated thicknesses where a uniform finish matters — laminate pressing, shuttering faces and furniture carcasses.

Bonding is the thing to get right. Interior furniture rarely needs more than an MR grade; kitchens, bathrooms and anything that will meet standing water want BWR or BWP. We quote the grade you ask for and mark the panels accordingly.

Thickness3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 16, 19 mm (others on request)
Sheet Size8′ × 4′ standard; cut sizes on request
GradesMR (commercial) · BWR · BWP / marine
CoreHardwood or combi core, as specified
FacesNatural veneer, film-faced or pre-laminated

Test certificates and grade markings are supplied with each consignment.

03 / Softwood

Pinewood

Pale, light and straight-grained — pine is the practical choice when weight, workability and price matter more than density. It takes nails, screws, paint and stain without fuss, which is why it dominates furniture, packing cases, mouldings and light interior work.

We supply sawn and planed pine in standard sections, kiln-dried and treated where the end use calls for it. Knot-free clear grades are available for visible surfaces; standard grades suit framing and hidden components.

  • Sawn and planed (PAR) sections
  • Kiln-dried to 10–12% moisture content
  • Clear and standard knotty grades
  • Pressure treatment available on request
Thickness19 mm – 75 mm
Width50 mm – 250 mm
Length6 ft – 12 ft
Moisture ContentKiln-dried, 10–12%
FinishRough sawn or planed all round

04 / Finished Goods

Home Decor Items

Finished wooden articles made by craft units we have worked with and audited — carved panels, turned and inlaid pieces, trays and serveware, planters, mirror and photo frames, and decorative accents for retail and hospitality buyers.

This line is built around repeatability. A decorative piece that photographs well once is of no use to a retail buyer; what matters is that the hundredth unit matches the sample. We approve a reference piece with you and inspect production against it.

  • Carved and turned wooden decor pieces
  • Trays, boards, boxes and serveware
  • Mirror frames, photo frames and wall panels
  • Custom designs to your drawing or sample
  • Private labelling and retail-ready packing

Finishes: natural oil, matt or gloss lacquer, distressed, or painted to a reference shade.
