Services

Nine services covering the full distance between a timber enquiry and a delivered container.

What We Do

Complete trade support

Engage us for the whole chain, or for the single step you would rather not manage yourself.

01

Export Services

We source, grade, pack and export timber, panel products and wooden articles to buyers across Asia, the Middle East, Europe and Africa — on FOB, CFR or CIF terms as you prefer.
02

Import Services

Import of sawn timber, logs, plywood and pinewood into India, including customs clearance, duty assessment and inland delivery to your unit or yard.
03

Vendor Sourcing

We identify and audit sawmills, panel plants and craft units against your specification, verify installed capacity and negotiate pricing on your behalf.
04

Quality Inspection

Species verification, grading and moisture-content checks at the mill, with photographic and dimensional reports issued before the container is sealed.
05

Custom Packing & Labelling

Export-grade packing, private labelling, barcoding and carton marking as per destination-market requirements and retailer norms.
06

Export Documentation

Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, phytosanitary, fumigation and ISPM-15 certificates — prepared and checked twice.
07

Freight & Logistics

FCL, LCL and air freight booked through established forwarders, with tracking updates from gate-in to final delivery.
08

Container Consolidation

Goods from several vendors combined into a single container, reducing your landed cost per unit and simplifying customs entry.
09

Sampling & Development

Sample development, costing and approval cycles managed end to end, before you commit to a bulk order.

Pan India

Local trade & distribution, Pan India

Not every order needs a container. Alongside our import and export business we trade domestically on a Pan India basis, supplying timber merchants, furniture and joinery units, panel distributors and project contractors across all Indian states.

  • Pan India supply to traders, manufacturers and project buyers
  • Despatch to all Indian states and union territories
  • Delivery by road, or collection ex-yard if you prefer
  • Part loads as well as full loads
  • GST-compliant invoicing and documentation

Common Questions

Before you send an enquiry

What information do you need to quote?
Product line, species or grade, the sizes you need, quantity (CBM, sheets or pieces), moisture content and finish, destination port, and your preferred trade term.
Do you supply samples?
Yes — timber and veneer samples, plywood cut-pieces and decor reference pieces. Courier freight is normally to the buyer's account and is adjusted against the first order.
What payment terms do you work on?
Advance payment, or an irrevocable Letter of Credit at sight through a reputed bank. Terms for repeat buyers are discussed case by case.
Can you handle a mixed container?
Yes — consolidation across several vendors into a single container is one of our regular services.
